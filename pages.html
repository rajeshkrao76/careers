About Us

Five years of finding the right people for the right roles.

Raveone Consultants is a Bangalore-based recruitment firm focused on MSME manufacturers, engineering companies, and specialist hiring across India.

Our Story

Raveone Consultants was founded with a simple belief: MSMEs deserve the same quality of recruitment as large enterprises — without the enterprise price tag or bureaucracy. For over a decade, we've partnered with manufacturers, engineering firms, and specialist companies to help them find, engage, and hire the right talent.

From CNC machinists to Sales Engineers, Quality Managers to Finance Heads, we've placed hundreds of professionals across India. Our strength lies not in volume, but in understanding — the shop floor, the sales cycle, the culture of an MSME. We speak the language of the people we hire.

What We Do

  • Permanent Placement — Mid to senior roles across engineering, manufacturing, sales, quality, and corporate functions.
  • Executive Search — Critical leadership hires with targeted headhunting.
  • Contract Staffing — Short-term and project-based technical resources.
  • Recruitment Consulting — Process design, JD writing, salary benchmarking.

Who We Work With

Our clients are MSME manufacturers and growing enterprises in industries like aerospace & defence, precision machining, plastic injection molding, packaging, electronics manufacturing, industrial automation, and specialty engineering. Many have been with us for years — a testament to relationships, not transactions.

Our Approach

We don't believe in "sending CVs." We believe in matching. Every candidate we submit has been screened against the client's specific technical blueprint and cultural fit. Our clients get shortlists, not stacks.

Our Process

A proven six-step recruitment process.

From understanding the role to the candidate joining — every step is designed to reduce time-to-hire and improve match quality.

  1. 1

    Discovery & Role Definition

    We start with a detailed call with the hiring manager. We document the technical requirements, team structure, cultural fit, and success metrics. If the JD needs refinement, we help.

    ⏱ 1–2 days
  2. 2

    Sourcing & Screening

    We source from Naukri, Shine, PitchNhire, LinkedIn, our internal database, and referrals. Every candidate is pre-screened against the role — no bulk forwarding.

    ⏱ 3–5 days
  3. 3

    Shortlist & Submission

    We submit a curated shortlist of 3–5 candidates with full executive profiles, resume, fit assessment, and availability. You receive ready-to-interview candidates.

    ⏱ 1 day after screening
  4. 4

    Interview Coordination

    We schedule interviews with the candidate, client, and panel. Reminders go out 24 hours and 2 hours before. We stay on top of confirmations and reschedules.

    ⏱ Within 2 days of shortlist
  5. 5

    Offer & Negotiation

    We coordinate offer letters, salary negotiation, and notice period discussions. We keep both sides aligned to close the offer smoothly.

    ⏱ 3–7 days
  6. 6

    Joining & Follow-up

    We follow up until the candidate joins — and beyond. Our post-joining check-ins ensure both candidate and client are satisfied after 30 days.

    ⏱ Ongoing
